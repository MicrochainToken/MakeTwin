# Project provenance and dependencies

MakeTwin builds on an earlier component-level Blender modeling toolkit and a mechanical showcase viewer, both developed by the project authors. Only reusable scripts, references, templates and generic tests/examples are included; product inputs, generated models, credentials and evaluation records are not packaged.

The component exploration controller and its metadata/state implementation were written for this project. No external machine-demo implementation, geometry, animation, sounds or GIFs are included.

The browser viewer includes Three.js and its associated helpers. Preserve templates/viewer/THREE-LICENSE.txt and vendor-sources.json when distributing the viewer. Manufacturer photos and manuals, if supplied in a product case, remain separate from this reusable Skill and retain their own ownership.
